Auto Video Uploader

Auto Video Uploader is a private, internal automation tool used by its owner to publish short videos to the owner's own YouTube channels and to read the performance statistics of those channels. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it does

  • Uploads videos that the owner has produced and approved to the owner's own YouTube channels.
  • Reads basic channel and video information and YouTube Analytics reports for those channels, to decide what to publish next.

Read the Privacy Policy to learn how Google user data is handled.
