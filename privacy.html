Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Auto Video Uploader ("the app") is a private, internal tool operated by a single individual (the "owner") for the owner's own YouTube channels. The app has no other users. This policy explains how the app accesses, uses, stores and shares Google user data.

Google user data the app accesses

The owner signs in with Google OAuth and grants the app these scopes, for the owner's own channels only:

  • youtube.upload — to upload videos the owner has produced and approved.
  • youtube.readonly — to read channel and video details (titles, IDs, view counts) of the owner's channels.
  • yt-analytics.readonly — to read YouTube Analytics reports (views, audience retention) of the owner's videos.

How the data is used

The data is used only to publish the owner's videos and to measure their performance so the owner can decide what to publish next. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not used to train AI models, and not used for any purpose other than these features.

Storage and protection

OAuth tokens and the statistics read from YouTube are stored only on the owner's own computer, in files that are not published or synced to any public location. Access is limited to the owner. Data is transmitted to Google only over HTTPS.

Sharing

Google user data is not shared with, transferred to, or disclosed to any third party.

Retention and deletion

Statistics are kept only as long as needed for the owner's performance reports. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; after revocation the app can no longer access the account, and the owner deletes the stored tokens and statistics from the computer.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy or requests about your data: open an issue at https://github.com/widodo2024/widodo2024.github.io/issues.

Kebijakan Privasi

Auto Video Uploader adalah alat internal milik satu orang (pemilik) untuk channel YouTube miliknya sendiri, tanpa pengguna lain. Aplikasi memakai izin Google youtube.upload (mengunggah video yang sudah disetujui pemilik), youtube.readonly (membaca data channel & video milik pemilik) dan yt-analytics.readonly (membaca laporan analitik video milik pemilik).

Data hanya dipakai untuk mengunggah video pemilik dan mengukur performanya; tidak untuk iklan, tidak dijual, tidak untuk melatih AI, dan tidak dibagikan ke pihak ketiga mana pun. Token OAuth dan statistik disimpan hanya di komputer pemilik dan dikirim ke Google hanya lewat HTTPS. Akses dapat dicabut kapan saja di myaccount.google.com/permissions; setelah itu token dan statistik dihapus dari komputer pemilik. Penggunaan data mematuhi Google API Services User Data Policy termasuk ketentuan Limited Use.
